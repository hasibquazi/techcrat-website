Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about our certified refurbished enterprise systems.

What does Grade A++ condition mean?

Grade A++ means the device is in near-immaculate visual and mechanical condition. It shows virtually zero wear, undergoes thorough physical sanitization, and passes a comprehensive 40-point diagnostic check covering the battery, logic board, ports, and screen by Techcrat Private Limited engineers.

Do you allow RAM and storage upgrades?

Yes, absolutely. Techcrat Private Limited maintains a large physical inventory of high-speed NVMe SSDs and dual-channel DDR4/DDR5 memory modules. We can configure and install these upgrades instantly at our service center.

Can corporate buyers claim GST input tax credit?

Yes. Techcrat Private Limited is a fully registered corporate entity (GSTIN: 19AAMCT5091M1ZI). We provide tax invoices containing your business’s GSTIN to ensure clean corporate auditing and straightforward credit filing.

Do you provide home deliveries in Asansol and Bardhaman?
